The table of contents lists the comments on the page (messages in AI chats) and the headings in them.

- Comments are shown as "`#number author (date)`". Gemini and ChatGPT don't show a date for each message, so only the author (You / Gemini / ChatGPT) is shown.
- As you scroll the page, the item you're reading is selected in the table of contents.

## Controls

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Left-click | Jump to that position |
| Right-click | Insert a quote (for a comment) or the heading text (for a heading) into the editor |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> + <kbd>T</kbd> | Toggle table of contents |

## Toolbar

The top of the table of contents has, from left to right:

| Position | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Left | Heading levels to show |
| Center | Three buttons: "Reload table of contents", "Go to top of page" and "Go to bottom of page" |
| Right | Buttons to change where the table of contents is placed |

"Reload table of contents" rebuilds the table of contents, not the page. The table of contents updates automatically; use this when it gets out of sync. Going to the top or bottom scrolls the whole page on GitHub, and the chat area on Gemini and ChatGPT.

## Placement

The table of contents can be placed in one of three positions:

- The edge of the screen opposite the editor
- Left of the editor
- Right of the editor
